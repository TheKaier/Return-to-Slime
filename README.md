# Return-to-Slime
You have conquered countless empires, you have ignited atomic fire, you have built countless wonders. But this time, your enemy is not another civilization. It is intelligent gelatinous beings. End them before the slimes take over the world, or become a piece of knowledge in their memory?你曾征服过无数帝国、你点燃过原子之火、你建造过无数奇迹。在史莱姆占据世界之前终结它们，或者成为它们记忆中的一份知识？
